# Coaching Real — campaign design DNA

Coaching Real contains distinct campaign landing pages. Each campaign may have its own visual identity, but it must stay conversion-led, editorial, accessible, and consistent across landing, signup, thank-you, and email handoff.

## Stuck in the Middle

- **Personality:** grounded, assured, humane, editorial, quietly authoritative, spacious, mature.
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond for decisive editorial display type; Manrope for navigation, body, labels, forms, and calls to action. Headlines use a tight 0.92–1.02 line height; body copy stays at 1.65–1.8 and no wider than 68 characters.
- **Color proportions:** soft cream `#F7F3ED` 48%; deep petrol `#234B4B` 30%; warm sand `#E8DED0` 10%; charcoal `#2D3030` 7%; terracotta `#C8755D` 4%; muted sage `#9EAD9B` 1%.
- **Spacing:** 8 / 12 / 20 / 32 / 48 / 72 / 104 / 144. Page gutters are 24px mobile, 48px tablet, and 72px desktop; content width is capped at 1240px.
- **Grid:** 12 columns. Hero uses a 7:5 editorial split. Content sections alternate between full-bleed bands and contained two-column compositions. Mobile collapses to one deliberate reading column.
- **Components:** restrained 1px rules, square-to-soft 4–12px radii, no glossy gradients, no floating glass cards. Terracotta is reserved for conversion and emphasis.
- **Motion:** `--ease-bridge: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`; 220ms micro, 620ms reveal. Motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Density:** one primary claim per section, short support paragraphs, structured evidence, and repeated enrollment points only after meaningful content.
- **Signature element:** a continuous “bridge line” travels through the page, becoming rules, week connectors, and CTA arrows. It represents carrying weight while creating a path forward.

## От експерт към онлайн бизнес — September 2026

Source: Stasi's supplied campaign documents and five portraits, read 11 September 2026. 23–27 September, 17:00–19:00 Europe/Sofia, €47. Existing campaigns remain intact.

Refero study: [Steep](https://styles.refero.design/style/75fdb89f-ca64-41b3-af36-7a78bd09448e), extended DESIGN.md and rendered preview inspected.
- Borrow: editorial hierarchy, restrained accents, clear separation between primary and secondary actions, 1200px reading frame.
- Translate: its quiet paper surfaces into campaign ivory/burgundy; use the existing Cyrillic Onest family, not Steep's proprietary fonts. Use 64–88px headline / 18px body and 64–88px section spacing.
- Reject: analytics imagery, peach brand palette, floating product cards, proprietary identity and copy.

Personality: assured, warm, practical, editorial, focused. Palette: ivory #F8F3EB 55%, burgundy #521B2B 30%, white 10%, charcoal and muted gold 5%. Source portraits retain their natural red/green clothing. Existing CR logo retained.
Typography: existing Onest with verified Cyrillic subset; hero 44–80px/1.02, section headings 32–52px/1.1, body 17–20px/1.7, 65-character reading measure. Labels 11–12px with modest tracking.
Spacing: 8/12/20/24/32/48/64/88. 24px mobile, 48px desktop gutters, 1200px max width. Hero split 7:5, one column on mobile. Portraits are original photographs, not generated replacements.
Components: thin gold rules, flat cream and burgundy bands, 4–12px radii, no decorative shadows. One main registration action, recurring after meaningful program information.
Signature: five numbered steps joined by a fine line; the same progression structures the curriculum and the outcome summary.
Motion: 220ms color/translate transitions with cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1); no hidden content, no autoplay, reduced-motion respected.
Content: preserve all five teaching topics and their concrete outcomes, the intended experienced audience, author biography, dates and €47 offer. Do not import past campaigns' guarantees, testimonials, recordings, scarcity or bonus claims.
