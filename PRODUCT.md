# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Bulgarian-speaking adults exploring coaching, professional development, specialist programmes, live courses, events, and community membership. Visitors commonly arrive from paid social traffic and need to understand the offer, trust the specialist, and decide whether to register or purchase.

## Product Purpose

Coaching Real presents Stanislava Pavlova's platform, specialists, programmes, events, and educational offers. The public site must turn attention into qualified registrations and purchases while clearly communicating who leads each offer, what participants receive, when it happens, and what the next step is.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, warm, confident. The experience should feel professional and premium without becoming distant, corporate, or theatrical. Bulgarian copy should be natural, direct, and easy to act on.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI landing-page patterns, loud web-red or orange, decorative clutter, weak contrast, fabricated testimonials or outcomes, fake scarcity, unsupported seat limits, and rolling countdowns without a real deadline. Do not sacrifice readability for visual effects.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next action unmistakable and keep ad-to-page message continuity.
2. Build trust through verified facts, real specialist imagery, clear delivery details, and transparent pricing.
3. Use authentic urgency when a real start date, deadline, price transition, or capacity limit exists.
4. Preserve the established Coaching Real identity while allowing each specialist offer its own art direction.
5. Check meaningful visual changes at desktop and mobile widths before publishing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, descriptive alternative text, semantic form labels, reduced-motion respect, and layouts that remain legible at 390px. Use natural gender-neutral Bulgarian for mixed paid audiences, while preserving explicitly gendered offer language when the programme is genuinely audience-specific.
